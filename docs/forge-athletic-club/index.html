01 — The floor

Five zones. Zero waiting for a rack.

Zone A

Strength floor

8 Eleiko platforms, 12 power racks, calibrated plates up to 50 kg and dumbbells to 60 kg.

Zone B

HYROX lanes

40 m sled track, SkiErgs, RowErgs, wall-ball targets.

Zone C

Boxing ring

Full-size ring, 10 heavy bags, pad work with ex-pro coaches.

Zone D

Recovery room

Ice baths, sauna, Normatec boots and a mobility studio.

Zone E

Private floor with its own racks and changing room. Open daily 9 AM – 1 PM and 5 PM – 8 PM.
