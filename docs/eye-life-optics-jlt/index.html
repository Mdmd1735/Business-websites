RTA eye test questions

Do I need an appointment?

Walk in any day from 10 AM to 11:30 PM (Friday 10 AM to 12 PM and 3 PM to 11:30 PM), or WhatsApp +971 50 923 6717 to book.

What do I bring?

Your Emirates ID, and your glasses or contacts if you already wear them.

Is the result sent to the RTA?

Yes, we're an RTA-approved eye test centre.
