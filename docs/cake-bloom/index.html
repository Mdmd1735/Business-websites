Ordering

Ordering a custom cake

How early should I order?

Call us at +971 4 344 6786 with your date and design. Lunchbox cakes are often available the same day; custom designs need more notice.

Can you write a message or copy a design?

Yes. Send a reference picture and the text you want piped.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver across Dubai. You can also pick up in Umm Suqeim 1.
