# Theme kits by category

Pick the kit, then **vary at least two** of: hero layout, type pair, palette, section order. Derive the accent from the logo or storefront colour in the photos.

| Category | Mood | Hero layout options | Type pairs (Google Fonts) | Palette starter | Must-have sections |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gym / fitness | Raw, kinetic | Full-bleed photo + giant stacked type · split video-style · diagonal cut | Big Shoulders Display/Instrument Sans · Anton/Inter Tight · Archivo Black/Archivo | Charcoal + one hot accent (orange, acid lime, electric blue) | Zones/facilities, timetable, memberships (AED), coaches, reviews, free-trial WhatsApp CTA |
| Martial arts | Disciplined | Centered crest + photo strip | Oswald/Source Sans 3 | Black, red, rice-paper | Programs by age, schedule, belts, trial class |
| Restaurant / café | Appetite, warmth | Food close-up split · menu-first · editorial collage | Fraunces/Work Sans · Playfair Display/Karla · DM Serif Display/DM Sans | Cream, terracotta, olive, or cuisine-specific | Menu with AED prices, signature dishes, delivery links (Talabat, Deliveroo), hours, map, reservation WhatsApp |
| Bakery / florist | Soft, crafted | Asymmetric image grid | Cormorant Garamond/Nunito Sans | Blush, sage, butter | Products, custom orders, gallery |
| Dental / clinic / physio | Calm, trustworthy | Light split hero with doctor photo + rating card | Plus Jakarta Sans/Inter · Manrope only | White, teal or navy, soft mint | Services, doctors + DHA/DOH licences, insurance accepted, booking, FAQ |
| Salon / spa / barber | Luxe | Dark editorial, slow image crossfade | Italiana/Jost · Bodoni Moda/Lato | Black + gold, or nude + espresso | Service menu with prices, stylists, gallery, booking (Fresha link if exists) |
| Garage / detailing | Technical | Diagonal panels, before/after slider | Rajdhani/Barlow · Chakra Petch/Inter | Gunmetal + yellow or red | Services + prices, brands serviced, before/after, location with landmark |
| Interior / furniture | Gallery | Full-width project carousel | Syne/Inter · Tenor Sans/Montserrat | Stone, walnut, off-white | Projects, process, showroom visit |
| Nursery / tutoring | Friendly | Rounded shapes, illustrated accents | Baloo 2/Nunito · Fredoka/Inter | Bright but limited (3 colours) | Programs by age, fees, KHDA rating, tour booking |
| Vet / pet grooming | Caring, playful | Photo of animals + paw motif | Quicksand/Nunito Sans | Teal, warm yellow | Services + prices, vets, emergency contact |

Animation menu (pick 3–5, not all): line-mask title reveal, scroll reveals, counters, marquee strip, review marquee, image hover zoom, parallax hero, magnetic CTA, tab transitions, before/after slider, sticky menu categories. Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Avoid the "AI look": no purple-to-blue gradients, no glassmorphism everywhere, no generic "Welcome to X" heroes, no emoji bullets, no identical 3-card feature rows, no Inter + gradient-blob combo. Write copy specific to the business (street, landmark, signature product, a line from a review).

UAE-specific: WhatsApp is the main CTA. Show prices in AED with "incl. VAT". Add an Arabic tagline or a toggle when the clientele is Arabic-speaking. Use landmark-based directions ("next to ADNOC, Street 8a"). Note weekend hours (Sat–Sun) and Ramadan hours where relevant.
